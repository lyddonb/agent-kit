# The quality books

These books hold the CI test records of the watched repos, booked by bstack's `qualitydirector` skill. This file is the rulebook, and it wins over the skill.

- The books under `books/` are written only by `quality.py`. Never edit them by hand.
- A weekly brief goes in `briefs/YYYY-MM-DD.md`, written from `briefs/TEMPLATE.md`.
- Questions for Beau go in `questions.md`.
